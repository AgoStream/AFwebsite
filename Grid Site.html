Research & Projects

MDN Training for KubeTwin

2025 — ongoing

Training Mixture Density Networks on performance traces collected from Kubernetes microservice environments. The goal: a learned component that reproduces the stochastic latency behaviour of a live cluster well enough to stand in as a digital twin. Latencies are modeled with a Weibull output distribution to better capture tail behaviour; traces are collected via Jaeger with controllable HTTP injection.
